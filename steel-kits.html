Barnhaus Steel Kits

Your Design. Engineered, Fabricated, Delivered as Steel.

Every Barnhaus plan can be delivered as a complete cold-formed steel framing kit — precision-fabricated wall panels and roof trusses built directly from your engineered plan set.

Request a Kit Quote  See the Process  Kit FAQ

What's in a Barnhaus Steel Kit

  • Engineered CFS wall panels — pre-assembled, labeled, and sequenced for your floor plan
  • Roof truss package — designed and fabricated to your site's wind and snow loads
  • Fastener & connection hardware schedule — everything needed to assemble the frame
  • Site-specific stamped engineering — sealed by a licensed PE for your jurisdiction
  • Erection drawings — panel-by-panel assembly documentation

What's Not Included

Foundation/slab, erection labor, exterior finishes (roofing, siding, windows, doors), and interior finish-out.

The slab-fit guarantee: we provide the foundation plan and anchor bolt layout your concrete contractor builds to — and we verify your slab as-built before your kit is released to fabrication. No verified slab, no fabrication. That's how we guarantee your steel fits your foundation.

Why Steel from Barnhaus

Because the same team that designed your home generates the framing package — directly from the construction model. No translation between your plan and your steel. Every stud, header, and truss is derived from the exact geometry you purchased.
