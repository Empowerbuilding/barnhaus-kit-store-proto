What's included in the Steel Kit?

Engineered CFS wall panels (pre-assembled, labeled, sequenced), the roof truss package designed to your site's wind and snow loads, fastener and connection hardware schedule, site-specific PE-stamped engineering, and full erection drawings.

How does kit pricing work?

Kits are quoted per project against current steel pricing — quotes are valid 30 days. A refundable $1,000 reservation locks your price and fabrication slot and credits 100% toward the kit.

Will the steel fit my slab?

Yes — that's guaranteed by process. You receive the foundation plan and anchor bolt layout before you pour, and we verify your slab as-built before your kit is released to fabrication.

Do plan purchases credit toward the kit?

Yes. 100% of what you've paid for this design's plan products is credited against your kit price.
