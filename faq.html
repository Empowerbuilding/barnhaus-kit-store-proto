Steel Kit FAQ

How much does a kit cost?

Kits are quoted per project — pricing depends on your design, site loads, and current steel market pricing. Request a quote from any plan page. Quotes are valid for 30 days.

Is the engineering included?

Yes. Every kit includes site-specific structural engineering sealed by a licensed PE for your jurisdiction.

Does the kit include the slab?

No — your local concrete contractor pours the foundation using the foundation plan and anchor bolt layout we provide. We verify your slab as-built before fabricating your kit so the steel fits.

Can I erect it myself?

Kits ship with complete erection drawings and labeled, sequenced panels. Many owners use local framing crews; we can refer experienced steel erectors in many regions. Erection labor is not included.

What if I already bought a construction set?

Your plan purchase credits 100% toward your kit.

How long does it take?

Typically 6–10 weeks from deposit to delivery, depending on engineering review and fabrication backlog.

Is the deposit refundable?

Yes — fully refundable until your kit is released to fabrication, and it credits 100% toward your kit price.

What about wind and snow loads?

Every kit is engineered to your site's specific loads. Some extreme-load jurisdictions may require additional engineering — we confirm before you commit.
