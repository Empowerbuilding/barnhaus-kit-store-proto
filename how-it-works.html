The Process

From plan to delivered steel — five steps

One team from design to truck. Typical schedule from deposit to delivery: 6–10 weeks.

1 · Choose your design

Start with any of our 75+ catalog plans or a fully custom design. Customize with our AI design studio. Already own a Barnhaus construction set? Your plan purchase credits toward your kit.

2 · Get your kit quote

We generate your framing package directly from the construction model and quote it against current steel pricing. Quotes are valid for 30 days. A refundable $1,000 reservation locks pricing and your fabrication slot.

3 · Site-specific engineering

Your kit is engineered for your exact site — wind zone, snow load, exposure category — and sealed by a licensed professional engineer for your jurisdiction. You receive the stamped set plus a foundation plan and anchor bolt layout for your concrete contractor.

4 · Slab verification & fabrication

After your slab is poured, we verify the as-built dimensions and anchor placement against the plan — then release your kit to fabrication.

This verification gate is how we guarantee your steel fits your foundation. It protects you from the single most common failure in kit construction: a slab that doesn't match the plan.

5 · Delivery

Panels and trusses arrive labeled, bundled, and sequenced with full erection drawings. Freight is quoted to your site. Many owners use local framing crews — we can refer experienced steel erectors in many regions.
